About Us — We Today

Engineering driven by constant innovation

We are engineering driven by constant innovation in our products and technologies, working in close cooperation with our customers' needs and future trends.

We have a global footprint and a global strategy, led by a strong central management team and deployed according to local needs — flexible enough to adapt to market demand.

We grow on two fronts: organically, by gaining market share in emerging markets, segments and technologies; and through carefully scouted acquisitions that consolidate our global position.

We build our business on continuous improvement, providing competitive and sustainable advantages for our customers as well as for ourselves. We are passionate, competent people.

We are Adler Pelzer Group™.

    Products & Technologies

    Materials, processes and products in one vertical chain

    We base product development on material selection, combination and improvement — controlling the full chain from raw material to finished component, with the same equipment worldwide.

    01 / MATERIALS

    Materials

    PUR foams, natural & man-made fibres, thermoplastic compounds and carbon composites — engineered for acoustic and thermal performance, with sustainability built in.

    02 / PROCESS

    Process

    Our patented CIM compounded injection moulding and HMP® hot moulding process deliver homogeneous density, lowest weight and up to 80%+ recycled content.

    03 / PRODUCTS

    Products

    Lighter solutions that improve acoustic behaviour while meeting fire, temperature and environmental targets — integrating more functions into every part.

    The full product range
