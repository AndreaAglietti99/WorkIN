About Us — We Today

Engineering driven by constant innovation

We are engineering driven by constant innovation in our products and technologies, working in close cooperation with our customers' needs and future trends.

We have a global footprint and a global strategy, led by a strong central management team and deployed according to local needs — flexible enough to adapt to market demand.

We grow on two fronts: organically, by gaining market share in emerging markets, segments and technologies; and through carefully scouted acquisitions that consolidate our global position.

We build our business on continuous improvement, providing competitive and sustainable advantages for our customers as well as for ourselves. We are passionate, competent people.

We are Adler Pelzer Group™.
