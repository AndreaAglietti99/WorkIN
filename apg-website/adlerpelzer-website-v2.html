    Innovation & R&D

    Engineering the Future of Mobility

    Our proprietary simulation and development tools allow APG engineers to design, test and optimise acoustic and thermal solutions entirely in-house — from concept to production.

    Simulation
    GENOME

    Proprietary full-vehicle acoustic simulation platform. Models sound transmission, absorption and vibration across all APG product zones simultaneously.

    Materials
    DNA

    Digital material database and selection engine. Indexes thousands of fibrous, foam and composite materials with predicted acoustic, thermal and weight performance.

    Process
    SPEA

    Smart Process Engineering Accelerator. Connects material choices to manufacturing feasibility, cycle time, tooling cost and CO₂ footprint in real time.

    Validation
    PROTEIN

    Performance Regression and Optimisation Tool for Engineering Innovation. Automated virtual testing against customer NVH and thermal targets before any physical prototype.

    AI / ML
    QUANTUM

    AI-powered topology and geometry optimisation. Explores millions of design variants to find the lightest, quietest and most manufacturable solution for each application.

    EV / BEV Ready

    Silence Reimagined for Electric Vehicles

    In battery-electric vehicles, the combustion engine masking effect disappears — every rattle, road noise and motor whine becomes audible. APG's BEV-specific solutions manage high-frequency e-motor noise, battery thermal management, and the new acoustic signature of silence.

    E-motor & inverter NVH isolation — targets 500 Hz–8 kHz frequency range

    Battery pack thermal & acoustic encapsulation — FireStop™ intumescent barriers

    Road noise dominance management — enhanced underbody, wheel arch and floor systems

    Cabin ambience — door, headliner and A/B/C pillar trim acoustic systems
